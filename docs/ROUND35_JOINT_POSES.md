# Joint geographic placement of compulsory components

A compulsory component pose fixes multiple labelled tile/rotation codes on a footprint of geographic cells. Two poses with the same footprint share their collision constraints and retain their distinct codes. The solver checks whether each footprint participates in a simultaneous disjoint packing. The remaining tiles must have a matching to vacant cells. Disjoint sets of admissible free owners use exact counts per set and unrestricted permutations inside them. Overlapping owner sets use alternating-path matching. A proved unsupported placement can be removed at an interrupted budget. Unresolved placements stay available.

The geographic matching CLI accepts `embed --joint-poses`. This Gold-only preprocessing is opt-in. Distinct labelled poses of the smallest eligible component can also be materialized as disjoint child domains. On four finite planted problems, their union contained exactly the independently enumerated Gold solutions, with no duplicate solution. The existing channeled geographic solver already decided all four in one call and zero branches.

The published board has eight compulsory two-tile components, 3,840 labelled root poses and 1,920 different component footprints. Every one has joint support at the unrestricted root. Four separately seeded geographic restrictions on those real components each lost 37 unsupported footprints, 73 labelled poses and 1,010 cell/rotation codes after independent component projection was already at its fixed point. Each restricted board still admits simultaneous packing. One disallowed domino footprint intersects both of a second domino's mutually disjoint choices. The alternate footprint survives. The JSON records the actual conflicting placements. The exact disjoint-pool check finished the root and all four restricted projections.

**Contour closure C2.** On published seed-zero walk and independent seed 0–15 streams, 32 emitted contours contain 31 distinct whole-board patterns, plus three redundant positive-control repeats of the first input. Every distinct contour admits jointly compatible required poses, without an additional placement exclusion at that stage. For each survivor, a reproducible owner matching defines a four-cell regional problem with every other donor fixed. All 31 such finite regions are impossible: each has an explicitly recorded mismatching seam between two fixed cells. The full incoming contours remain eligible for different donor assignments. On the held-out ring there are nine distinct contours, one previously excluded by a mandatory component, seven impossible four-cell residuals and one independently validated Gold residual. The raw evidence distinguishes each source, full pattern hash, scoped certificate and repeated input.
