# Pricing odd sets of physical strand teeth

A comb row is valid for a Hamiltonian circuit when its odd number of disjoint teeth each touches both sides of its handle. For two-strand teeth the fixed-handle price is a bipartite cardinality matching with weights `2*x[u,v]-1`. The benchmark tries every odd cardinality still capable of a violation. It also considers connected teeth of size three or four, retaining every transition multiplicity of its physical mate. Integer min-cost or set-packing prices propose candidates. The installed row, if any, is repriced directly with the original, unrounded strand LP values.

The published physical LP reached its pre-existing cut plateau after 62 strand cuts and 141 odd physical-side cuts. There were still 755 fractional strand edges. We screened 160 additional handle partitions and 22,804 connected two-, three- and four-strand tooth sets. All fixed-handle two-strand prices fell beneath the necessary simple upper bound. Of 117 larger-tooth packing problems surviving the corresponding bound, none yielded a violated row. Three original 12-, 48- and 96-pin residuals added 480 handle screens and 380 larger-tooth packings. Twelve separately seeded planted profiles added 685 handles and 390 packings. None of these 887 final packings exceeded the proof-or-retry budget, and none produced a new CP row. Two planted strand projections already described one integral cycle.

A five-tooth fractional prism is an adverse control. It survives every proper subtotal boundary of two and every two-strand triple. Exactly two complementary handles expose its violated five-tooth row. Independent enumeration verifies the reconstructed inequality on all 362,880 rooted ten-vertex Hamiltonian orderings. The fixed-handle prices also agree with exhaustive disjoint matching at all eligible cardinalities on 120 independent small weighted graphs. The broader diagnostic remains off in the solver by default. Its demonstrated original workload would send identical constraints to the native integer search.

Reproduce with `python -m unittest test_r35_comb_pricing` and `python bench_r35_comb_pricing.py --tiles data/tiles.json --rounds 256 --family root`, repeating the benchmark with `fixed` and `planted`. The decision covers the generated handle families and connected teeth of at most four strands at these 16 separated LP points. Different fractional solutions or a proposed larger-tooth witness can reopen it.
