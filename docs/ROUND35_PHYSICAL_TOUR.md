# A single circuit on the physical Gold strands

The published puzzle has 365 physical strands. A directed circuit can assign all of them a single successor cycle before a board location is chosen. Each physical mate Boolean still controls every occupied slot on that joined side. Its two possible directions use that tile’s signed phase. Supports giving the same successor arc are combined by logical OR. The contracted model joins already compulsory paths before building the circuit. Two remaining paths can legitimately be joined twice. A proper closed component excludes its entire sewing.

**Original result.** In the unrestricted physical relaxation with blank projection, raw and contracted circuits each found a single-loop sewing in one native call. Raw took 78,903 branches and contracted took 78,428. The previous lazy solver remained open after four calls, 123,369 branches and 27 distinct strand cuts. The newly saved full sewings each include 240 reciprocal physical pairs and one independently traced 365-strand cycle. Their ribbon genera are 35 and 37. Every geographic root fails for both. These are physical parents for subsequent exchange experiments. Neither is a Gold board. The original root model shrank from 365 to 345 circuit vertices and from 5,272 to 5,190 arcs by contraction.

Use `python r30_gold.py matching --project-blanks --tour-mode contract --seconds 60` to search for physical sewings. The optional `raw` mode skips contraction. `sparse` posts the contracted circuit up to 256 successor arcs, covering the measured small finite models. It disabled the circuit on the original root and reproduced 123,369 branches. A small-arc threshold is unsuitable as a substitute for the explicit contracted mode on the unrestricted physical comparison.

On four held-out ten-tile projected physical scopes the lazy arm took 3, 3, 2 and 1 calls. All circuit profiles needed one. At seed 157 the baseline needed only 63 branches versus 290 contracted. On seed 103 the contracted model took 2,987 branches versus 2,245 despite saving a call. Independent tracing of four finite families admitted 56 of 64 physically complete sewings. Raw, contracted and sparse CP, plus a separate fixed-phase SAT position model, each returned exactly those 56. The checked SAT diagnostic has an explicit arc-by-position size limit.

**Geographic comparison C1.** The original narrowed board fixed every tile identity to its 207 reference cell and left all three rotations free. Physical-plus-geographic checks, the geographic port circuit, complementary parents/flows, and both coupled parent/flow plus strand models all rejected it at zero branches. All methods also rejected the five-loop Euler-admissible board and the split planted scope. On the planted positive the existing geographic channel and geographic port circuit returned verified Gold at zero branches, versus five in the coupled channel. Adding the physical circuit to parents increased 160 to 166 branches, or 138 to 153 for flow. For the archived 217 nonblank bonds, every model/phase profile admitted the abstract tour in one call and rejected all 480 geographic roots in one call. Keep the physical circuit for physical search. The default geographic channel and existing complement models remain the established choices at their measured scopes.

Reproduce with `python bench_r35_physical_tour.py --tiles data/tiles.json --output docs/R35_PHYSICAL_TOUR.json --save-tours docs/R35_CONNECTED_TOURS.json` and `python -m unittest test_r35_physical_tour test_r30_exactgold test_r31_gold`. The JSON contains the two new physical sewings and the separate geometric, phase and SAT comparison results.
