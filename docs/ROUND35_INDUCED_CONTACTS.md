# How many of the 207 reference bonds can Gold keep?

**Every Gold board must drop at least 31 of the 207 named physical bonds in the 52-component published reference. It can retain at most 176.** The exact signed-inventory relaxation attains 31 with the explicit tile-color phase and physical losses recorded in the receipt. Constructing a full one-loop board from those choices remains a separate search. The highest verified complete board remains at 207 matched edges. [Exact proof receipt and witness](R35_CONTACT_WIDE_PROOF.json)

## Running the bound

From the installed project root on Windows, the first command independently checks the physical scope and its signed witness. The second uses the bound for the unrestricted signed query. The third applies it to an actual geographic Gold search. The geographic option helps most when many specific bonds are already compulsory. The portable dashboard’s fresh-board search has a different objective and entry point.

```powershell
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe verify_r35_contact_wide.py --tiles data\tiles.json
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe r32_gold.py integer-bound --tiles data\tiles.json --all-groups --claims docs\R35_GEOGRAPHIC_RESIDUALS.json --claims docs\R35_EDGE_CONTACTS.json --upper 30
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe r30_gold.py embed --tiles data\tiles.json --reference-bound
```

For ranked signed search, use `r32_gold.py loss --all-groups --refine-integer` with the same claims. At 30, both public signed receivers returned infeasible with zero search decisions. At 31, both repriced the published witness before returning it, and the ranked search opened zero pages. A genuinely distinct signed query can use `integer-bound --integer-backend scip --independent-native --seconds 0` with its own loss cap and claims. ## Physical premise and proof

A set of retained physical seams may place a rigid group of tiles on the bipyramid in several ways. If every placement brings two other sides together and their raw line endpoints disagree, every edge-perfect board must break one of the original seams. The receiver enumerates root positions and all three proper rotations. It keeps this distinct type only when the group has a geographic embedding and every embedding has an unavoidable mismatch. Pure physical pairing and pose-only searches cannot consume the stronger rule.

The first two packable donor components had ten proper placements apiece and forced 27 and 26 mismatches. Their paths supplied 19 verified rules. The full incumbent scan covered all 2,143 simple paths through length 17 between its 33 mismatching contacts. After excluding dominated paths, 1,750 needed an explicit check and 966 additional rules survived. The resulting 985 include the original 19, one of which has length 19. The independent scalar checker reads actual segment endpoints in every reconstructed assignment. [Replayable contact rules](R35_EDGE_CONTACTS.json)

The global proof needs 13 previously verified physical groups, eight compulsory seams, 194 geographic groups and 326 of the induced-contact groups with at most 13 premises. Removing compulsory premises and duplicates yields 528 distinct obligations on optional bonds. The signed model includes 43 redundant cycles individually checked as parity contradictions. Each chosen physical loss is counted once even when it satisfies multiple obligations. The equality witness also satisfies every one of the 985 published contact groups.

The exact SCIP run used 372 integer variables, 1,083 linear constraints and 7,225 branch-and-bound nodes. The release verifier independently reconstructs all 1,827 VIPR premises, including all 744 variable bounds. The solver’s 4,732 weak linear rows were completed using explicit previously proved global bounds. The unmodified upstream VIPR checker accepted all 70,980 resulting derivations. At 176 the extra geographic work exhausted its short 0.4-second budget on a sample that cheaper checks could close. Leave the option off for unrelated fresh boards.
