# Rotated physical placements and witnesses

Round 35 workstream 30 uses the verified proper rotations of the canonical mesh that preserve the actual pose domains. Distinct physical tiles make the preimage of cell zero sufficient to select the lexicographically least orbit image. Pose offsets are still necessary when returning a scored witness. A noncanonical mesh or an asymmetric fixed pose retains its original coordinate frame.

Full-shell center selection can collapse ten rotated presentations of one center into a single candidate and reuse one proved movement-budget cap. The selected board keeps its original coordinates and piece labels. A coordinate-specific ranked interval retains the individual centers, because rotating a support can change its colex rank. With `r30_multicenter.py --orbit-scores`, an exact ordinary seam optimum saved for a rotated endpoint can be queried from the existing SQLite table. The runner inverse-transports its poses, checks that every physical tile is in the requested cell, and independently validates its seam score and components before returning it.

An audit of the installed 16 fresh CPU/GPU starts, three published centers, 4,573 saved exact permutations, 6,207 legacy optima, 236 queued plateau vertices, 56 expanded vertices and 310 legacy centers found 10,568 distinct physical permutations in total. Every one had a different proper-rotation orbit. Scanning those SQLite files was read-only. The audit supplies no basis for imposing orbit keys on every plateau queue entry or moving coordinate-specific path ancestry. Finite controls explicitly transport a two-cell step and its inverse through all ten coordinate frames. A pose-pinned mesh preserves its distinct keys. Exact orbit probing is opt-in.

The planted full-edge witness moved from support rank zero to rank one under rotation. A direct run used one native exact query and the transported run used zero. Both independently replayed the 15-edge single loop. For the real fresh archives, exact posed-board mask caching priced eight boards on the initial eight-board read, eight more when expanded to sixteen, and zero on a third full read. A changed pose receives a new mask key. All sixteen original inputs remained excluded at the tested 208 cutoff.
