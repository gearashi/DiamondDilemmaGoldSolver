# Exact small contacting repairs and full-model replay

## Checked replay

`r31_fresh.py --joint-cache` stores only attained local optima with a solver proof. The local model includes every donor, rotation, cell domain, changed-contact condition, physical forbidden side and external score contribution. The key serializes the full labelled numerical factors after ordering the slots and donors and expressing each rotation relative to the previous piece at its destination. Replaying a matching key restores the chosen codes on the current board. It checks physical legality, contact and mate restrictions and the complete score delta. The bounds of 256 entries and 8 MiB apply to raw key and witness payloads. Partial CP answers and timeouts do not populate the proof cache.

In the four random single-contact and four guarded single-contact 320-round runs, complete queries did not repeat. Four independent eight-cell forest/disjoint runs skipped 12 entire CP models and 12,945 branches. Eleven of these hits had an identical original physical description. The fully labelled numerical model recovered one additional equivalent residual. The four no-contact regional controls already bypassed all 1,280 CP calls using #36. All sixteen final physical hashes matched the uncached controls. The largest actual per-seed payload was 203,778 bytes. A stopped 36-round forest run resumed to 64 rounds with the same codes, score and work counters as a cold 64-round run, crossing a 1,048-branch duplicate. Its compressed checkpoint field contained 284 Base64 characters. The optional cache receipt binds its full image and work counters to the physical scope. A rotated planted query reused an exact positive optimum and recovered the known Gold witness.

## Certifying new queries that have one contacting pair or disjoint changed contacts

For at most twelve cells, the solver can price every single changed cell and every pair of changed neighbors with all legal donor rotations. Single changes include their incident old neighbors. A pair removes both unilateral charges on its common seam and substitutes the true joint score. Compatible separated units then add exactly. A donor price cancels over any complete permutation. If every single and allowed pair has nonpositive reduced gain, the current board already maximizes this local objective.

`--joint-bound dual` applies that root certificate. `supports` also checks the possible sets of changed cells, using only the original donors belonging to each set. `exact` solves the remaining loose sets, prevents two units from spending the same donor and restores the rotations attaining the best feasible gain. It uses pruned donor-subset layers for small forests. For non-disjoint, one-contact pools of at least ten cells, repeated remaining-unit and donor subsets can reuse a proved maximum or a tagged upper bound. A lower target reopens a formerly insufficient bound. The option falls through to the ordinary regional model for unsupported overlapping contacts. An induced geographic matching is within scope even with a higher requested contact limit. Zero-gain closures preserve the incoming physical board. Equally optimal alternative boards can lead to different later heuristic trajectories.

On 384 fresh comparison queries and 256 pools of the installed 207/54 board, an independent validator built unit gains directly from complete incident seam scores. It checked every root price inequality or independently enumerated physical donor placements. Of 640 queries, 596 closed at the root, 16 more closed through unit upper bounds, and five more closed after exact donor accounting. The remaining 23 had a strictly improving placement totaling 35 seams over their respective input boards. Sixteen of those optima beat their bounded CP witnesses, by 24 seams summed across those queries. The zero-gain cases originally consumed 664,291 CP branches; 282 had returned with only a feasible status or no conclusion.

All 256 installed 207/54 queries returned the unchanged board. They covered 128 distinct random and 64 distinct forest pools after combining seeds. A separate 256-query check preserved the shipped 207/52 reference on 128 distinct random and 60 distinct forest pools. All 256 had directly verified root duals; their CP controls took 260,831 branches. These results apply to the listed donor pools. They leave the seven/eight-piece route ledgers and wider geographic search intact.

The unpruned exact subset method charged 383,473 retained subset states on the 640-query comparison. Donor-adjusted suffix bounds reduced this to 95,929. Ordering entire supports by highest upper bound alone worsened it to 125,962. Smallest supports first used 93,343 states, and breaking equal-size ties by their upper bound used 93,284. Unconditional cross-support exact memoization used 98,948 newly solved states. The retained selective version combined 77,228 threshold-aware subproblem visits, 108,845 previously proved bounds or maxima reused, and 422 ordinary subset states on forests. These are internal regional work counters. The native weighted-tail alternatives traded states for older assignment machinery. The one-pair version required 11,820 extra weighted problems and examined 373,260 legal input arcs, so it stays a comparison option in the benchmark.

All 3,840 were independently checked on the actual boards reached. The weaker 64-round ablations skipped 697/768 CP calls using the root alone and 738/768 with changed-support bounds. The NumPy-only Python 3.15 profile also completed the eight-cell forest command on the original input, reaching 161 in sixteen rounds.

**Decision.** For an exact small fresh-board repair with one contacting pair or disjoint changed contacts, use `--joint-bound exact`. Keep `--joint-cache` for repeating proved local CP models, especially outside the exact contact scope. The fresh trajectory comparisons support different settings on different starts. No original case exceeded the established 207 board or reached Gold.
