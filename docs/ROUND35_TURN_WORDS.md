# Mixed turns on physical tile polygons

A fixed path consumes an incoming and outgoing side at each internal physical tile. The difference gives an oriented left or right turn. A simple cycle of named tiles must return to its starting cell and incoming side with no intermediate cell repeated. The geographic matching CLI now accepts `embed --turn-length 10`. It checks a candidate seam closing a path of fixed mates and installs exact bounded polygon conflicts from a trial sewing. Lengths 3 through 16 are supported. Constant-turn corner rings retain their existing separate check. A traversal budget can leave additional polygons untested.

The table keys include turns up to cyclic shift and reversal with sign inversion. Every start cell and heading is replayed once per canonical word. Domain-restricted polygons additionally check the actual identity and rotation of every tile on each surviving walk. A row with no compatible walk yields only the clause over that polygon's selected physical seams. The table lives on the query's mesh.

The entire published mesh admits four of the 135 mixed word classes of length three through ten. None exists below length eight. On the 60 published fixed-bond scopes, 19 had already closed by side matching. Among the survivors mixed turns excluded 81 choices and closed one scope. One choice at 48 pins remained after the existing corner check. Its complete labelled replay is in the accompanying JSON. The 85 priced polygons used 26 distinct words and reused 59 entries. All recorded removals and the closure were independently checked by named physical pose enumeration. The checked 217-bond connected abstract sewing has 41 independently unembeddable mixed polygons through length ten.

On planted scopes 35, 71, 103 and 157 the new arm found and validated Gold in 4, 7, 7 and 3 calls. The physical matching baseline used 13, 34, 40 and 9, with the 40-call scope still open. The existing fully channeled geographic solver decided all four in one call and zero branches. Keep this option for sparse or projected physical matching. The default remains the existing geographical channel.

The exhaustive control compares every labelled turn word of lengths three through seven and its reversal and cyclic shifts with named component pose enumeration. It also tests hypothetical closing seams, 250 seeded anchored-domain cycles, positive full boards, the constant-turn boundary and a zero-work interruption. Reproduce with `python -m unittest test_r35_turn_words` and `python -m benchmarks.bench_r35_turn_words --tiles data/tiles.json --output docs/R35_TURN_WORDS.json`.
