# Geographic reference retention across alternative placements

The published Gold reference has 207 matched bonds. The checked loss certificate limits retention to 187 of them. A candidate placement can force one of several different reference bonds even when no single bond is globally forced. The existing `r30_gold.py embed --reference-bound` preflight now counts these alternatives before constructing the native geographic model. Its use remains scoped to authentic near-reference Gold searches.

Each optional bond contributes at most two accounting units in one complete board. A possible implication at both endpoints receives one unit per endpoint. A bond that has just one represented endpoint can charge two there. Cell and tile minima give an initial lower bound on additional retentions. A weighted tile-to-cell injection on the remaining charged cells can strengthen it. The solver reconstructs an integer assignment dual and validates every inequality. A zero-cost choice count sufficient to accommodate every other charged cell removes that row from the projected minimum. For more than 32 surviving rows, the preflight uses its cheap bounds. A positive dual can identify a smaller group of competing cells through a maximum zero-cost matching. Repricing only that group allows a full charge for a bond whose other endpoint falls outside it. The two certificates are used independently. Dual prices can also remove individual placements, including uses of a tile required elsewhere to meet the budget.

**Original measurements.** On 292 unrestricted or near-incumbent scope/retention combinations, the additional assignment minimum was zero. The 217 single-pose exclusions beyond the old saturation threshold occurred in scopes already covered by earlier physical conflicts. Those observations support leaving the geographic bound optional on unrestricted fresh searches.

Constructed scopes on the published pieces used two spatially distant alternatives to distinguish the new proof. Across four seeds, four motif families and two retention levels, 16 of 32 scopes exceeded 187, including eight where every individual pose fit the spare allowance. None contained a previous departure clause. In the public paired control, one CP presolve call and zero branches became zero native calls. In a separate four-seed family, four distant cells shared one uncharged physical tile. Each paid alternative forced two reference labels. At 185 individual retentions the cell and tile minima were zero, and no single pose exceeded the budget. A four-row checked assignment dual established at least three extra bonds and closed all four. The public previous implementation again made a native call and exhausted in presolve.

Across 40 wider allowance checks on three- and four-cell competition, 24 closed at the root. Conditional dual prices removed 453 placements in each of eight surviving three-cell cases and 444 in each of four others with four competing cells. Repeating the full check after those 12 reductions produced zero further cuts. In separate tests 160 random four-tile incidence/domain systems were enumerated over every labeled legal placement under three loss budgets. The release also checks a positive sibling that charges both ends of one reference bond, a case where equally good choices retain different bonds, and physical edge-perfect residuals. The relaxation refers to forced named bonds. It makes no assertion that its feasible assignments complete Gold.
