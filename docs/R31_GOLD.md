# Gold assembly experiments

Every verified Gold result requires 160 distinct physical tiles on the original mesh, 240 matching seams and one loop. Planted controls use their own inventories. The shipped round-29 physical permutation and original tile data give a new lower bound. **Every Gold board must break at least seven of the 207 matched physical bonds on the saved 207/52 arrangement.** A bond names two physical tile sides and follows those tiles if they move together. This bound measures lost bonds. It permits several bonds to break in one move, and applies only to Gold.

The experiment replayed 231 distinct signed-inventory XOR conflicts from the 207 permutation and extracted seven with disjoint sets of physical bonds. Every Gold assembly must break a bond in each set. The minimum hitting-set search had a time budget, and the seven disjoint certificates are independently valid regardless of the hitting-set incumbent. The eight earlier 320-round fresh winners gave respective lower bounds of 4, 5, 4 and 6 for CPU and 4, 6, 8 and 6 for CUDA. Each witness lists physical side IDs in the [machine-readable evidence](R31_GOLD_RESULTS.json).

| Proposal | Test and disposition |
|---|---|
| 20. Geographic conflicts | With an unembeddable side matching, test its cyclic side ordering for positive genus and use disjoint pose packing to shrink the conflict. Unselected bonds stay free. Training on 59 unembeddable ten-tile matchings produced verified conflicts of 2–7 bonds. These ruled out 1,623 of 2,421 different held-out matchings before full embedding. Every skipped matching was separately checked. The geographic solver reads saved conflicts after reproving them on the current domains. |
| 21. Bidirectional geographic support and fragment recovery | The mesh-seam tables link adjacent physical placements to a single selected physical bond. The ten- and 160-cell planted residuals each found Gold with one CP call and zero branches. Previous final embedding stayed open after 32 calls on both. The exact table is attempted on residuals within its size limit. On the unrestricted 160-tile root the limit leaves the original model active. On a distorted fifteen-bond matching, geographic salvage recovered a legal seed retaining 13, with both required bonds included. |
| 22. Necessary bond departures | Signed-parity explanations were rechecked from the named physical bonds alone. Disjoint witness packing and minimum hitting-set optimization select bonds to release. The original-scope results are above. A capped hitting-set solve reports its feasible suggestions separately from the proved lower bound. |
| 23. Fractional separation | A fractional side matching with bundle-preserving strand flow proposes tour cuts. Also tries odd sets of up to fourteen candidate sides in each self-reciprocal mask class and three disjoint two-strand teeth around selected fractional handles. On the four-matching negative control three strand cuts lowered the integral solve count from two to one. Every exclusion comes from CP-SAT with structural integer inequalities. An LP status alone never closes a scope. No extra odd-set or comb benefit appeared in the retained comparison. Two 12-second original-root runs stayed open. The experimental LP proposed twelve strand cuts there, without a full-board exclusion. |
| 24. Exact two-crossing cuts | Prove at most two crossings, impose the necessary two crossings, then encode a single path in each side with shared physical seam choices. One negative residual had 34 possible bridge bundles of which 26 failed cheap joint checks. The final model closed in one CP call versus two. Replaying the hinted cut recomputed its capacity under current assumptions. Two nested cuts simultaneously retained an independently verified planted Gold. |
| 25. Both complementary regions | Contract permanent seam intervals and require at most two connected components in the selected placement's complement through two rooted forests or two sources of flow. Oppositely colored apices are the roots when the verified odd-segment condition applies. Noncrossing strand geometry and at least one strand ensure the complement has at least two regions. The split and nested negative controls closed at presolve with zero full boards, versus three and one respectively for completed-board pocket cuts. The parent and flow models remain optional residual checks. |
| 26. On-demand vertex rings | Price oriented rings from dual values, keeping both ends of each physical side tied to one mate. On the planted scope both methods examined 68 successors and found seven columns. On the original scope both reached the 100,001-successor cap. The generated subset does not certify infeasibility. The explicit recheck optionally runs the independent complete integer ring census, and stays unknown when it caps. The test provided no advantage for pursuing deeper integer branching on priced columns or enabling this by default. |
| 27. Integer phase interfaces | Track exact signed reciprocal balance, self-reciprocal parity and Euler terms. Equal residual tuples merge. A compiled boundary can answer narrower queries including new boundary pins without replay. On the original inventory with 148 phase pins, 222 transitions compiled four boundary phases. Two different child queries needed zero transitions and both agreed with independent CP. The unrestricted original query exceeded the 512-state allowance. |
| 28. Switches from disconnected covers | Join cover components temporarily to form an auxiliary Euler circuit, then apply circuit nullity to all strand switches belonging to the same physical seam trade. On a three-loop ten-tile sewing a single trade recovered the independently verified planted geographic Gold board. Randomized bundled switch families also matched direct exhaustive rewiring. This requires a complete compatible physical sewing, a phase assignment and disjoint nonempty switch bundles. The original experiments supplied no appropriate geographically recoverable cover. |

## Running the checks

Use the full OR-Tools Python environment and the Gold tiles generated by setup. From the release directory run the following commands.

```text
python r31_gold.py departure --input docs/R29_INCUMBENT.json --output runtime/gold-departures.json --seconds 10
python r30_gold.py embed --input residual.json --output runtime/assembly.json --seconds 30
python r30_gold.py embed --input residual.json --reuse runtime/assembly.json --seconds 30
python r31_gold.py salvage --input fragments.json --output runtime/salvaged.json
python r31_gold.py phases --input phase-interface.json
python r31_gold.py parents --input residual.json
python -B -m unittest -v test_r31_gold test_r30_exactgold
python bench_r31_gold.py --tiles data/tiles.json --output gold-benchmark.json
```

The embedding command enables geographic channels within the size limit and learns geographic conflicts. The restricted matching and embedding commands accept `"fractional": true` and `"bridges": [[...]]`. Each bridge is a proper subset of physical strand IDs in tile/segment order. A saved result includes recheckable geographic exclusions and bridge hints. Controls use `"channel": false` and `"learn_geography": false` to disable those additions.

Salvage requests use `required` and `bonds` arrays of physical side pairs, and optional per-cell `domains` arrays of placement codes. Mark all globally mandatory bonds as required when creating a forced-bond fresh seed. The departure result also supplies disjoint branches by the first broken bond of its shortest conflict. Pass the departure JSON to the matching or embedding command with `--reuse` to rederive the XOR proofs and require at least one lost bond in every accepted core. Add `--departure-branch N` to select a zero-based first-broken-bond branch. The CLI independently rechecks the shortest physical core, requires all earlier bonds in that core and forbids the selected one. Completing all its branches covers all Gold boards. A scoped answer identifies the selected branch. A real six-bond core was handed to the CLI twice. Its first branch required zero earlier bonds, and its last required the preceding five. Both time-limited original searches returned unknown. A feasible salvage result contains complete `codes` usable as a hint. The departure command accepts a complete board's `codes`; `trials` changes the number of seeded XOR-certificate attempts. A phase-interface request can batch `queries` with individual `pins` and `boundary` tile lists. Only completed projections enter the in-process cache. The `rings` command accepts `"recheck": true` to run independent integer verification. The `switches` request uses `bonds`, one phase per physical tile in `phases`, and `trades` naming two physical sides whose current partners will be exchanged. The optional benchmark arguments `--fresh runtime/fresh-cpu.sqlite --fresh runtime/fresh-cuda.sqlite` score those existing 320-round checkpoints read-only.
