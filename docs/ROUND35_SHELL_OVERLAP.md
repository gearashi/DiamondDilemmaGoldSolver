# Exact separation and overlap of typed exchange shells

Round 35 workstream 31 computes the number of common **labeled physical permutations** of two specified cycle families in a fixed relative orientation. The Hamming screen, transposition triangle inequalities and parity are first checks. For a relative permutation with small moving support, donor subsets on a directed cycle are equivalent under cyclic shifts. Permuting equal-length cycles preserves the permutation. The implementation counts each representative with its exact multinomial weight. A binomial coefficient counts the choices of labeled donors among the unchanged positions outside the difference support. Each represented left order is composed with the other center's inverse. Fixed-point counts of successive powers determine its cycle type. A zero count excludes the center pair for that family before endpoint generation. Domains can further restrict a possible intersection. The count is an upper bound if additional pose restrictions matter.

Across all seven labels and smaller controls, complete permutation enumeration agrees for every pair of nontrivial classes, including unequal moved-set sizes. Extra eligible labels, repeated equal cycles, and cases surviving both parity and transposition distance are covered. For several earlier centers, the selector prices their actual union when the common changed-label calculation fits 500,000 order/stratum/copy checks. The larger fallback remains a sampling order heuristic and never proves disjointness. On a seven-center pinned fixture, all 21 novelty queries used exact counts. Independently materializing every endpoint found exactly the 45,616 distinct boards predicted by the incremental counts.

For the published outer centers, raw overlaps were 4,279,650,440 (six movers), 115,097,857,875 (seven) and 883,293,846,435 (eight). An independently enumerated third-center exclusion removed respectively 1,431,190,020, 38,414,671,295 and 294,431,282,145 endpoints in previously covered neighborhoods. The remaining 2,848,460,420, 76,683,186,580 and 588,862,564,290 sum to the independently recorded **668,394,211,290** eligible shared endpoints.

Of 684 center/family pairs from the existing real starts and published centers, twelve copies survived Hamming, eight were already excluded by parity and the remaining four intersect. A separate comparison constructed 22 distinct legal original-puzzle center pairs which survive the older tests but cannot meet for the requested family. On one complete donor support per pair, all 720 formerly repeated ownership classifications for 2+4, 520 for 3+3, and 210 for 2+2+3 disappeared with the same negative result. Among two four-pair shells no additional distance/parity survivor at the tested 160-cell width was disjoint by cycle type. These constructed cases measure use beyond the installed starts, without claiming a score improvement on the incumbent.
