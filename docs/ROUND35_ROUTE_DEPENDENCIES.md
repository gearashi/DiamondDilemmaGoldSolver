# Typed ownership dependencies and checked rank ranges

One completed geographic support page certifies its entire interval once all earlier-owner dependencies have been satisfied. The reader now caches checked intervals and walks the dependency graph iteratively. A missing or cyclic dependency leaves its caller open. A genuine 120-link chain became provable after the last page arrived, and an injected cycle stayed unresolved.

New multicenter pages retain the actual transformed permutation family for each deferred endpoint alongside the original legacy union. The reader limits a proof to the requested source family and, for sparse pages, the originating support rank. A page exceeding 4,096 distinct deferred links retains the full family union. Older publications keep the conservative untyped dependency rule. Owner-specific positive maxima and the requested threshold are still checked. A targeted positive in the other family cannot exclude a covered negative, and a genuine positive in the required family blocks transfer.

All 245,760 owner/rank points of 240 applicable 1,024-rank receipts from the installed four-swap ledger were verified. Reading each owner point separately previously required 245,760 proof-row queries. Verified interval reuse required 240 reads and answered the remaining 245,520 lookups from memory. These historical pages had zero deferred links. Typed dependency validation used a separate finite two-center mesh, reconstructing all 6,072 shared labelled endpoints against independently transformed physical permutations.
