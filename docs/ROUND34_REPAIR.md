## 5. Shared defect-cover certificates

The stream of typed donor prefixes now retains checked infeasible CP assumptions as sets of seam-failure clauses. A clause can transfer only when the current obstruction involves the same geographic failure edges and a subset of its eligible movers. Both the mover and failure budgets must be no larger. This permits reuse after changing fixed donors, center, pool or target when the actual obligations still imply the cut. The cache retains up to 1,024 cores in each run. Positive and interrupted solves add no negatives. Finite cross-center tests compare every answer with a fresh solver and cover missing rows and both changes of budget direction.

Across all 80 published prefix records, three configurations per record used the full movable pool (3 extra movers, target 208), cells 0-79 (3, 208), and the full pool (2, 209). Reusing checked cores avoided 98 of 240 CP calls. The 142 remaining calls learned 4,105 clauses in total; 195 of 240 optimized queries closed. With a four-second CP limit, nine paired controls contained at least one UNKNOWN and all other pairs agreed with fresh solving.

## 6. Exact mover-quota multipliers and reduced costs

## 8. Small and bipartite swap conflict components

With `r31_fresh.py --exact-packing`, connected positive swap conflicts of up to 22 vertices use exact independent-set search. Larger bipartite components use a weighted minimum cut when OR-Tools is available. In the remaining large components the same highest-priority greedy swap is fixed and the residual is split again. Independent tests enumerate all subsets on twelve-vertex problems and demonstrate a larger bipartite cut beating greedy. On 48 random original-puzzle starts (seed 834, alternating full board and 30-cell pools; last 24 received twelve independent-set repairs), 49,841 positive swaps were priced by each version. The new pack gained eight extra matched seams across eight cases, and never decreased any case's score.

## 14. Soft-score seam hitting-set master

The experimental `r34_seam_master.py` models failures as a hitting set. Its restricted donor and cycle subsolver enforces the other geographic seams with every rotation free. Every returned core was checked again with unconditional seam constraints. A cut transfers only to more tightly pinned donors in the same model. Finite controls exhaustively enumerated both sides of the true maximum and rejected the reverse pin transfer.

Cold target-208 runs on the published 207 center (one swap from pools `0,1,2,3`, `4,9,16,19,22,43`, and `2,6,9,17,43,66`) required twelve physical calls, twelve independent replays and 25,976 physical branches. Direct CP proved all three in presolve with zero branches. Raising the target to 209 reused four cuts per pool with no new physical calls, but the lower target's negative already entails that result. Retained as a reproducible experiment only; the app continues using direct CP for this scope.

## 15. Regional component swap budgets

`r30_bounds.py component-budget --candidates ... --swaps 4 --min-moved 7 --nodes 100000` separates local donor responses whose affected stars do not overlap. The component prices include legal physical donors and a mask for all donors used. Max-plus combination permits incoming identities from other components without reuse. Each cycle spanning components crosses at least twice; this gives a lower bound on the number of swaps, while cycles entirely within a component have exact cost. A computational budget returns the existing coarse ceiling. Warm queries share component tables across thresholds and avoid the join entirely on equal swap budget and mover floor. `r31_routes.py --component-rows 100000` can use the result to publish checked ranked support coverage.

In 36 eight-cell pools on the original center (Random seed 1534), all budget-three ceilings equaled unrestricted breadth-first enumeration. The component method priced 17,504 local donor rows versus 83,196 full permutations, plus 89,824 compatible donor-mask join probes. Thirty-four ceilings tightened relative to the coarse defect bound. With zero minimum movers the identity placement has a relaxed star score of 208, so it alone prevents this score relaxation from establishing a lower ceiling. Requiring at least seven movers for the budget-four route closed all 36 regions at 208, 35 additional compared with including all smaller supports. In 24 direct runs over twelve of these pools (both families), the optional component filter published the same 108 ranked supports and best results while avoiding 8,789 cycle extensions and 1,148 catalogue rows. It spent 23,456 local prices and 67,414 cheaper joins, so leave it optional. Controls include a positive crossing-donor swap, deliberate interruption, brute regional enumeration and warm/cold identity.
