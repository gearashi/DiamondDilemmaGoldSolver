# Live route coverage and independent root pages

Round 35 workstreams 26 and 27 use the same rank, cycle family, physical center, domain and compatible target as the original searches.

## Live cross-traversal evidence

Each connection checks SQLite's data version and its own write counter at page boundaries and at most ten times per second during pricing. Completed negative intervals survive these generations. A missed lookup expires when another worker commits. This permits ranked, finite-pool, streamed-prefix and connected-root workers to consume compatible results after startup. A cross-center negative must account for its actual transformed owner and family. An outstanding dependency, a source above the requested threshold, a partial root and a donor outside the recorded pool all leave work open.

The finite runner also recognizes an entire root pool after every canonical root has been completed without a target witness. In the reverse direction, at most 2,048 checked support ranks are tested for a family-wide negative before opening its root traversal. Larger pools still check individual surviving endpoints. A positive stored for a coarse ranked page can fall outside a smaller finite pool. Asking the finite runner about that rank now calculates its local answer and retains it in a separate singleton record, preserving the aggregate and its witness.

On the original 207 board at target 208, completing the 140 roots of the published eight-cell 3+2+2 pool closed all eight support ranks. Conversely, reading eight completed ranks closed all 140 roots with zero cursor steps instead of 308. On an eight-cell four-pair pool, one completed rank closed 28 roots and avoided all 4,543 cursor steps. Root-first completion avoided 2,660 and 1,170 standalone cycle prices on the two finite jobs. Each reverse pass polled the commit generation once. The direct searches on those pools produced no 208 witness.

## Fenced cursor updates

A worker leases an individual root, performs cursor advancement and rotation optimization outside the writer transaction, then compares the saved stack and best under the lease before publishing. It can advance an independent available root. A stale owner cannot overwrite a successor. Independently verified exact physical scores survive an interrupted cursor page. Reopening completed roots omits construction of the join, price catalogue and rotation oracle. A failed worker can repeat the uncommitted portion of its own cursor page.

Two overlapping public workers on the same original intervals performed precisely the serial 6 and 691 cursor steps. A third connection acquired the SQLite writer while the first worker was held inside cursor advancement. Reopening the two completed roots performed zero cursor steps and zero join/oracle constructions. Concurrent workers can independently build their own small catalogues, as recorded in the paired JSON.

Finite adverse controls force a genuine checked positive, interruption immediately after its score transaction, an expired worker after another has reclaimed and completed its root, an unrelated aggregate witness, and an arriving cross-owner dependency. The controls compare physical witnesses, completed rows and independent serial coverage. The synthetic positive tests explicitly compare connected-only subtrees on their planted meshes. The two original benchmarks use their published connectivity proofs.
