# Shared exact coverage decisions

## Per-scope invalidation of symbolic coverage (66)

**Reject for the current solver’s normal workload.** The production publisher in `constraint_work.CoverageLedger.record_completed` inserts new records with `INSERT OR IGNORE`. A source search for updates, deletions, replacement inserts and table drops on `closed` found them only in tests of deliberate external modification. The existing scoped sequence already handles concurrent inserts. Its global epoch advances only on a destructive change.

In two independent SQLite connections, a 10-cell binary box had 1,024 assignments. Eight assumed exclusions under Gold left 1,016 assignments, verified by explicit enumeration. An edges-only writer inserted 128 distinct boxes, tried all 128 again, and reopened the Gold diagram every 16 new records. The epoch stayed at zero, the original diagram was built once and reused eight times, and every remainder matched. An intentional out-of-band deletion of one edges record advanced the epoch once and triggered one Gold rebuild with the same 1,016 answers. That control is an opportunity only for a future normal editing/compaction workflow. There are zero ordinary rebuilds here for a per-scope epoch to remove. Keep the existing deletion and corruption regression tests.

## Explicit portable transfers (67)

**Keep for transferring pending search work in the version-1 box format.** Selecting portable frontier export writes disjoint merged boxes when their union is exact. A validated complete partition may collapse to its full Cartesian product by cardinality. For a remainder with holes, two groups can merge only if all their other cell domains are equal. Continue until no such siblings remain. The native checkpoint retains its original groups, cursors and owners. A GPU-to-GPU restart needing the same partition should use the native checkpoint.

On seven completed matched transfers to CPU DFS, the same independent scopes took **120 versus 51 DFS nodes** in total. A planted 160-cell Gold witness survived a merge from 27 boxes to one. The six-cell prism shrank from 59 to 20 imported roots and from 69 to 29 DFS nodes. Persistent SAT made 137 versus 60 native calls, but SAT decisions increased in aggregate from 142 to 189. On the 12-cell word-boundary control they increased from 36 to 103. Compaction is useful for transfer size and repeated root setup; do not enable it as a general SAT branching heuristic.

Without the inventory screen, 32, 64, 128, 256 and 512 original-board roots each merged to one. At 128 roots the serialized transfer shrank from 2,592,869 to 60,235 bytes (9,955 to 445 bytes at the benchmark's zlib setting). With the recommended inventory screen, the 81 surviving original roots at 128 lanes had no compatible siblings. The saved original DFS and hybrid frontiers likewise stayed at 19 and 18 boxes. A fresh GPU import repartitions as required by its lane count.

The tests compare actual assignments for all 512 subsets of a 3-by-3 grid, all 256 subsets of a binary three-axis grid, and 60 additional mixed-width partitions with codes above bit 128. Re-importing a verified compact cover is idempotent. Corrupt checksums and overlapping rows with a recomputed checksum fail before compaction. Application tests verify that implicit GPU exports retain the previous raw representation. Reproduce the 19 measured inputs with `python -m benchmarks.bench_r35_portable_siblings --tiles ../data/tiles.json --runtime ../runtime --output docs/R35_PORTABLE_SIBLINGS.json`. The optional runtime directory supplies the two saved frontiers. [Measurements](R35_PORTABLE_SIBLINGS.json)

## Parked native GPU archives (69)

The released runner validated, split and recompressed waiting GPU lanes on each resume. The first candidate carried already aligned, verified archives. The second also carries intact archives of different widths. The GPU assembles the requested width when those lanes are dispatched. The on-disk format is the existing native format. Every contributing parked archive still passes mechanical validation before the runner accepts its counters or portable frontier.

On an eight-lane original-board checkpoint, nine successive resizes to 2, 2, 3, 3, 1, 1, 4, 4 and 8 lanes re-encoded 41,462,256 bytes with v1.15.0, 18,846,480 with aligned reuse, and **8,480,916 with deferred assembly**. The corresponding counts were 26, 12 and 6 encodings. All three validated 35 archives. Every active and parked lane matched the released version byte-for-byte at each stage, including its owned root and cumulative search counters. Peak traced host allocation during a single original resize was about 19.9 MB, 19.9 MB and 18.0 MB, respectively. This measurement covers traced allocations during restore.

On a second eight-lane input, independent enumeration found six edge solutions. They were recovered at all nine widths. Four actual searches then ran through the normal dispatch path to an edge witness or exhaustive Gold negative. The two policies launched identical device widths, reached the same answers and used the same node and seam-support counts. For the one-lane Gold negative, encoding dropped from 20,972 to 8,988 raw bytes, while decoding rose from 44,940 to 53,928. Known fork, donation, incremental and partially retired fixtures were also replayed. Tampered, mechanically invalid or incorrectly counted parked lanes were rejected.

**Keep deferred assembly and a 128 MiB host cache for verified parked CUDA batches.** The cache retains arrays that were already inflated and validated in the same solver call. A cold reopen still checks every contributing archive. The cache can reuse the verified bytes on dispatch, including when a parked group is split again. Hash, binding and mechanical checks still run on activation. No raw cache is written to disk. [Three-arm native evidence and dispatch counts](R35_PARKED_ARCHIVES.json)

Seventy matched budget, target, width and early-pause configurations ran on the RTX 2060. On the original 160 pieces, a 156-fixed residual included an unavoidable mismatch between cells 6 and 31 (ports 6 and 7). All 48, 128 and 256 lanes of their respective searches closed. At the usual 128 lanes, a zero-cache completion processed 286,466,496 bytes through the decoder. The chosen limit processed 120,617,472 bytes, all from the first validation. It retained at most 113,078,880 bytes. The 512 MiB control avoided zero additional bytes. All settings returned the same terminal checksum and work counters. Immediate pauses had zero cache hits, with identical saved checksums.

At 256 lanes, the chosen limit reduced decoder input from 467,392,704 to 339,236,640 raw bytes. A 256 MiB limit reached the cold minimum of 241,234,944 by retaining 226,157,760 bytes. The 512 MiB arm tied it. The default bounds the additional live host storage to roughly 0.8% of the tested machine's 16 GiB RAM and eliminates repeat inflations in the recommended 128-lane control. These RAM/decoder measurements apply to resumed parked work. The fresh root search performs no additional pruning. An altered compressed frame must be decoded anew, and a modified binding or state hash fails. The wire format, legacy factored import, and ownership boundaries remain covered by the paired controls. ## Selective coverage lookup (65, opportunity screen)

For 128 individual queries over 64 disjoint, independently infeasible boxes of the original puzzle, filtering by four restrictive cells selected 64 of 8,192 proof payloads. Their total size was 2,084,870 bytes versus 266,863,360 bytes. Grouping eight queries left 603 of 1,024 payloads. The full frontier and unrestricted queries selected every record. On the six-piece Hall fixture the four-cell filter selected 274 of 1,121 records for single live boxes. On original unrestricted Hall coverage there was only one reusable proof. These counts measure selection before retrieving full payloads. They establish demand for the storage experiment. Actual database reads, integrity, simultaneous writers, adverse inputs and ordinary versus symbolic consumers remain under test. [Scope and query matrix](R35_COVERAGE_SELECTIVITY.json)

The measured database experiment compared four paths through the normal remaining-work entry point. Each of the 128 original single-box queries returned the released ordered residual. The release-style stream checked 6,176 rows (200,810,097 proof-column bytes), an index on four previously authenticated axes checked 127 (4,128,987 bytes), and a bounded cache of complete checked boxes stored as differences from the target root checked 64 (2,080,774 bytes). The existing symbolic solver checked 64 and represented the same assignment set. Complete checked boxes also avoid reparsing a positive hit. The minimum here is one cold delivery of each of the 64 original payloads. The two one-time broad queries each checked 64 in every mode. [Actual SQLite payload and independent subtraction comparisons](R35_COVERAGE_READS.json)

The ordinary SAT session completed the same eight native calls and eight disjoint closures across eight interruptions. Checked proof payloads fell from 60 to 11. The checkpoint and remaining-work tests included an external writer committing in the middle of a read, epoch invalidation after a delete or corrupt update, caller-owned rolled-back SQLite sequence reuse, an old unindexed database, and a damaged proof at a position outside the pending hull. A persisted selector can conceal this last case if it is trusted across process restarts. The implemented cache authenticates rows on first encounter in each process and bounds its in-memory prefix. A cold read on the RTX 2060 also closed the same saved CUDA work with zero additional search nodes. [SAT and six cache budgets](R35_COVERAGE_CONSUMERS.json)

The larger test published 512 proofs that share 156 fixed cells and read them from the original unrestricted scope. Nine queries checked 3,332 rows when streamed, 2,285 at a four-MiB cache budget, and 512 at 16 or 32 MiB. The stored differences then occupied an estimated 12,329,704 bytes. Different proofs repeatedly store the same fixed-cell restrictions. This is a decision-changing memory question. ### Final coverage decision

Keep the process-private cache of complete verified boxes for ordinary repeated reads. Store small IDs for shared masks and sparse changes from the root or a checked dense row. For mostly fresh wide masks, retain the literal box or a checked patch against it. Either of two independently sampled coordinate sets may suggest a literal base. Reconstructing a patch compares every coordinate. A collision can change only its size and admission. Committed rows are checked before entering the cache. A reader with an open external transaction uses the ordinary validated stream. The symbolic reader retains its own exact representation.

In 16,384 disjoint partitions of a restricted original board, nine lookups encountered 8,192 independent proof rows. One MiB required 18,627 payload checks with dense anchors and 34,205 without them. Four MiB reached the cold minimum of 8,192 with 1,394,056 estimated retained bytes. For 8, 32 and 128 interleaved original families, the four-MiB results also reached the cold minimum. The authenticated source-root representation needed substantially more memory for the same answers. [Original 8,192-proof crossover](R35_LARGE_COVERAGE.json) · [Wide and sparse comparisons](R35_COVERAGE_FINAL.json)

An adverse control fixed the two endpoints of one known mismatch on the original puzzle while generating unrelated 480-bit domains for the other cells. At four MiB, the literal fallback held 271 of 512 unrelated proofs versus 118 with mask IDs alone. At eight MiB it held all 512, using an estimated 7,915,520 bytes. With 256 KiB and eight interleaved families it held 292 versus seven. A single candidate literal anchor regressed on clustered input. Testing two candidate bases recovered that case, holding 324 clustered proofs versus 256 without the fallback. Set the lazy ceiling to eight MiB per reader. At sixteen MiB the measured full-coverage cases had no further payloads to avoid. In a separate allocation trace, the dense unique-mask cache held 541 proofs and retained 8,371,648 traced bytes. The short-lived peak including the attempted next row was 8,399,168 bytes. [One-anchor rejection](R35_COVERAGE_ENTROPY.json) · [Final adverse sweep](R35_COVERAGE_ENTROPY_TWO.json) · [Python allocation audit](R35_COVERAGE_ALLOCATIONS.json)

The actual two-worker CPU coordinator on an independently enumerated five-piece ring exhausted all remaining work with both readers. The final matched pair visited 97 nodes and completed 14 jobs in each run. Checked payloads fell from 1,606 to 29. Dispatch timing can vary the number of queue reads. The fixed SAT and original-board queries provide deterministic counts. Retain the checked stream for cold broad reads and the unread suffix when the budget fills. A persisted unchecked selector failed the damaged-cold-row control. Reopen this choice if a normal consumer changes the trust model, or if a measured recurring production stream exceeds eight MiB and its rereads justify a different retention policy.
