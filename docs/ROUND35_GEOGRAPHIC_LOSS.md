# Checked reference-loss constraints in geographic Gold

The published reference has 207 matched physical bonds. A separate cap-19 SAT refutation for the signed inventory and ten replayed disjoint departure groups establishes that every geographic Gold board must omit at least twenty of these bonds. Its complementary cost-20 phase witness certifies the relaxation optimum. The original archive contains the CNF, DRAT proof and checker transcript, and the raw seam/group/required-bond reconstruction is recorded in R35_LOSS_PROOF.json. The geographic receiver regenerates all 207 physical pairs, eight required pairs and the ordered conflict packing under the puzzle fingerprint, then compares the digest with the exact certified scope. It has no user-supplied certificate or loss number to trust.

Use `python r30_gold.py embed --reference-bound` for a near-reference domain or explicit retained bonds. The solver checks both possible owner directions and counts each physical bond once, even when occupied cells or rotations can move. More than 187 required retentions closes the residual before allocating the native CP model. At 187, every other reference mate is forbidden and placements known to imply one may be removed. Otherwise the full physical model receives the cardinality constraint. Blank-side projection falls back to the full model when counting individually named blank pairs. Foreign drawings and moved references without the exact same physical reference scope get no certificate.

Twelve seeded reference samples at each of 186–189 explicitly retained bonds contained none of the thirteen verified individual conflict clauses. Twenty-eight of the 48 samples nevertheless exceeded the proven limit after compulsory mates were included. Twelve reached equality and each excluded the twenty optional original pairs. In the measured 188-retention native comparison one presolve invocation became zero, with zero solver branches in both. The 187-retention native comparison already closed in presolve in both arms. The incumbent's region with twelve free cells retained 190 pairs and closed. With twenty cells free the same construction had 172 fixed pairs, below the cap. The original unrestricted physical tours retained only 38 and 35. Leave this option off for fresh unrestricted boards.

Tests compare every edge-perfect placement of three finite labelled residuals under randomly chosen reference subsets and limits, including equality and lower-weight witnesses. Additional controls reject a changed physical reference, missing groups, an untrusted scope and an inapplicable objective.
