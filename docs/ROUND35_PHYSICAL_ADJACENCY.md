# Geographic tile neighbors

The published mesh has three different neighboring cells at each face. The physical placement of any tile can therefore use another tile at most once. The filter groups selected seams by tile pair and checks whether the two remaining sides have a joint choice of neighboring tiles. It derives the maximum from the actual closed mesh, including multiplicities two and three. Open and self-attached meshes bypass this rule. The classic 160-cell GPU engines already require three distinct reciprocal neighboring cells on input. All Gold mate restrictions, including the earlier mandatory partners used as premises, are isolated from the ordinary edge objective.
