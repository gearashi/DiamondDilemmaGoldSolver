# Interrupted five-swap order catalogues

Round 35 workstream 28 keeps a scalar cursor for each expensive surviving support, center and cycle family. It binds the exact surviving sequence, uses mixed-radix random access for a full family, and persists cumulative first cuts, typed deferred owners and the best independently checked witness. An active support lease must still pass its fence before each partial save and before final promotion. A partial support never increments ranked coverage. The final page absorbs the saved work and removes the partial records in one transaction. Preexisting complete support pages retain their original meaning. The result can expose a positive witness during a partial run.

The default quantum starts at 128 orders. A short slice can double up to 512 and an expensive slice can halve down to 16. Family sequences smaller than 512 or twice the requested quantum use the existing whole-support path. The public runner and CLI accept `order_quantum=0` and `--order-quantum 0` respectively to reproduce the earlier behavior. Separate workers retain their normal support leases. The experiment identified restart replay within one expensive support; it did not establish a need for concurrent ownership of that same support.

On the original 207 board, the default star screen rejected all 2,520 orders of the first nine-cell support before enumeration and wrote no order cursor. Disabling that earlier screen for the interruption comparison exposed the full catalogue. Stopping after 587 endpoint classifications and resuming cost 3,107 checks without a cursor and 2,595 with a cursor in the recorded run. It recovered 512 previously classified orders from a 75-character payload, excluding SQLite keys and page overhead. Both runs recorded 2,513 predecessor rejections, seven endpoint rejections and no target witness. Adaptive slice boundaries can vary with machine load.

A held-out two-center fixture stopped after 3,301 classifications. It recovered 3,288 complete orders, taking 5,053 total checks in place of 8,341. Both routes retained identical typed dependencies, first cuts, and the verified 15-edge planted Gold witness. Every promoted support reopened with zero classifications. Other controls kill a child process immediately after its first fenced write, expire its old lease, resume with a changed quantum, stop again on a physical positive and reopen it through the public API. A stale writer and a changed checksum or catalogue cannot supply the frontier.

Reproduce with `python bench_r35_order_frontier.py --tiles ../data/tiles.json --output docs/R35_ORDER_FRONTIER.json` and `python -m unittest test_r35_order_frontier test_r34_repair.FiveSwapTests`.
